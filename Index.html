Questions, answered.

Does it work with iPhone and Android?

Compatible NFC-enabled phones can interact with the card. Some devices may need NFC turned on in settings.

Does the customer need an app?

No additional app is required for the basic NFC experience.

What happens when someone taps the card?

The card stores a web URL, which opens the destination page on the customer’s phone.

Can you program the card to our Google review page?

Yes.

Can the design have our logo?

Yes. Customized versions can include your business branding.

Can I order multiple units?

Yes. Bulk orders can be arranged.

Can I use it for something other than Google reviews?

The card stores a web URL, so it can potentially send customers to other online destinations as well.
